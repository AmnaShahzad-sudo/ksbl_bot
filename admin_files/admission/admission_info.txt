6. GUIDELINE TO RULES & REGULATIONS
ADMISSION STATUS
All admissions are provisional unless students have submitted all the required documents and KSBL has verified those documents. Required documents submission time is four weeks from commencement of the first semester. All students whose admissions have been confirmed will be notified.

ADMISSION DEFERMENT
A student who has secured admission at KSBL can defer his/her joining for one semester after paying the admission fee and the security deposit and submitting the complete documents required for admission. Duration of the Academic Program for such students shall start once they join the program.

What is the deadline for admissions?
Ans. The deadline to apply for Fall 2024 (Round 1) is April 14, 2024.
----------
When will the admission test be conducted?
Ans. The admission test for Undergraduate Programs will be conducted on Wednesday, April 17, 2024, and for Graduate Programs, Sunday, April 21, 2024.
----------
What is the admissions process?
Ans. Undergraduate Programs:
- Online application submission
- KSBL Undergraduate Admission Test (KUAT)
Graduate Program:
- Online application submission
- KSBL Management Admission Test (KMAT)
- Interview
----------
Where does KSBL conduct admission tests and interviews?
Ans. The test and the interview are both conducted on campus.
----------
Is the KSBL admission test compulsory for every student seeking admission in KSBL?
Ans. No, it's not. Candidates for graduate programs can opt for other tests in place of KMAT including GMAT, GRE, and NTS-GAT. Candidates for the undergraduate programs can claim an exemption for KUAT based on a valid SAT score. However, the payment of the admission application processing fee is compulsory and remains unchanged in case of an exemption.
----------
What is the eligibility criteria for admission?
Ans. The eligibility criteria is as follows:
For Undergraduate Program: 
- 12 years of Education at the time of Admission (Those awaiting result may also apply)
- This includes HSSC (min. 60% marks), A-Levels (Minimum one B and 2 Cs) or Equivalent & IB

Graduate Program:
- 16 years of Education at the time of Admission (Those awaiting result may also apply)
- Undergraduate CGPA 2.5 or more
- KMAT/GMAT/GRE/GAT General
- Must have 5 years of professional experience required (Only for Executive MBA)

Ans.  KSBL doesn't have any preference for the preparation, however, students can take help from the online sample paper and general practice material available online.

Is work experience required for an MBA?
Ans: KSBL MBA is open for fresh graduates. Five years of work experience (post-graduation) is required for our Executive MBA Program.
